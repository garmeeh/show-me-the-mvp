# Show me the MVP

A founder pastes an idea and gets back the smallest version worth building, plus a test that proves or kills it.

## Language

**Idea**:
The app, product or business a founder pastes in, in their own words, before any stripping back.
_Avoid_: Prompt, pitch, concept

**MVP**:
The smallest useful version of an Idea that could be built or shipped to prove it.
_Avoid_: Prototype, v1, minimum product

**MVP Brief**:
Our response to one Idea: the MVP, who it is for, what to build first, what to cut, the Riskiest Assumption and the Success Test.
_Avoid_: Analysis, report, result, teardown

**Riskiest Assumption**:
The belief that, if false, kills the Idea: the thing the Success Test exists to check.
_Avoid_: Risk, hypothesis

**Cut**:
A tempting feature deliberately left out of the MVP, with the reason it can wait.
_Avoid_: Nice-to-have, backlog item

**Success Test**:
A question answered by real behaviour that proves the Idea or kills it, such as "Can 10 restaurants get real customers to place orders through it?", with the bar that counts as a pass.
_Avoid_: Metric, KPI, validation criteria
